# Game Trend Radar UI

## Visual direction and provenance

An original, content-first game discovery interface. The calendar opens immediately, followed by upcoming picks and recent releases. A warm cream field, sparse radar marks, dotted texture and large cropped orbital shapes give the backdrop depth. The upper color field curves into the page, with honey, mint, peach, lavender and sky themes for the calendar, upcoming, released, saved and date pages. Lavender and mint patterned sections, cobalt controls and clean reading surfaces retain a playful identity without a promotional hero, carousel or promotional summary counters. A shared type scale, restrained shadows and consistent card actions connect the site.

- `assets/radar-mark.svg`, `assets/radar-pattern.svg`, radar decoration, heart icon, CSS and UI text are authored for this project. The abstract pattern uses original circles, arcs, dots and strokes; it is a decorative CSS background with no hit targets or accessibility-tree content. No Nintendo/Pokémon logos, characters, illustrations, videos, sounds, screenshots, fonts or source code are included.
- **Noto Sans TC** is loaded from Google Fonts with `display=swap`; installed system fonts are the fallback. Its upstream license is [SIL Open Font License 1.1](https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/notosanstc/OFL.txt). The repository does not bundle font binaries.
- Text uses six sizes from `src/shared/styles/type-scale.css` (`--text-xs` 12px up to `--text-2xl` 32px); nothing on a page is smaller than 12px. Only display numerals above 32px and fluid `clamp()` headings keep their own size. Every link and control is at least 24×24 (WCAG 2.2 AA); back links, small text links and disclosure toggles are 44px tall, and a growth ranking row opens its game from anywhere in the row. `tests/browser/readable-targets.spec.ts` checks both on every public page at desktop and phone widths; the internal scheduler page and its timeline dots are not covered yet.
- Game covers continue to use the Steam asset URLs provided by the existing dataset. Their rights remain with their respective owners. Those assets are distinct from the original site interface; this redesign does not grant additional rights to third-party game artwork.
- The reference websites informed general principles (clear hierarchy, cheerful color, generous imagery and responsive feedback), not reusable artwork or distinctive compositions. These implementation choices are not a legal clearance opinion.

## Interaction

- Global navigation contains 發售月曆, one 遊戲探索 disclosure and 我的收藏. The disclosure links directly to 全部遊戲, 即將上市, 近期上市 and TAG 探索, followed by 關注成長榜 and 遊戲比較（實驗）. Original calendar, rocket and tag drawings live in mint, peach and lavender menu entries, with a matching blue catalog icon. The current destination is highlighted. Native details/summary works without JavaScript; the small navigation module adds outside-click, focus-leave and Escape dismissal, arrow-key navigation, and a closed state on history restoration. The menu respects reduced motion. There is no exploration hub or duplicate child-page navigation; the old `discover.html` URL redirects to `games.html`.
- `games.html` searches the full accepted public catalog, including retained older releases and qualified recent-source records. It does not apply the 45-day or 30-day view windows, and does not expose unqualified backend candidates. Title/AppID/official bilingual TAG search combines with date period, actual supported language, follower and local favorite filters. Filters persist in the URL, and opening a profile retains the filtered list as the return destination.

- The single-day page is titled 每日發售遊戲. A lavender toolbar groups the complete year/month/day between circular previous/next controls, with a weekday badge alongside (below on phones) and a native date picker for direct jumps. It has no current-month button or calendar/list switch. Both stepping and jumping reuse the loaded catalog, preserve filters and browser history, and keep the date picker and return-to-calendar month synchronized.

- The home calendar opens first. Previous/next buttons, the current-month button and a native month picker keep the calendar and URL synchronized. Phones default to a card list with an explicit calendar/list switch.
- The entire card opens the site's `game.html?appid=...` profile. Favorite and visible Steam-store links remain separate controls above the card link; Steam opens in a new tab.
- The profile (2026-11-19) opens on Steam's full-width library hero (`data/media/<appid>.json`; console games and games without media keep a theme-colored band with original radar rings) and a title card that overlaps it with the name, genres, the large release date, a countdown, the store link and the local favorite. Below it, in order: in-page links to the sections that have content (sticky on desktop), 雷達快報 (dated radar records: activity events, the latest Followers change, the pre-launch date confirmation, Steam media updates), the published description, 玩法一覽 (genres, highlight TAGs, verified play modes, Steam language support), 影音 (a silent Steam microtrailer that plays in view on desktop only when motion is on and data saving is off, and screenshots that page through the image dialog), 熱度 (the latest Followers, 7/30-day changes over their real day spans, a chart of measured days that breaks after 8 days without a measurement, and the Twitch archive matched by Steam AppID or an exact console name), the fixed-format info card (cover, names, developer/publisher, play modes, platform, Taiwan Steam price with any discount and the time Steam confirmed it, CSRR rating with its descriptors and Steam Deck status from `data/store_facts.json` when Steam has them, release tickets, platform editions, languages, AppID and data notes) and similar games. The general structure follows the order of console game pages (key art, news, story, features, product facts); artwork and layouts are not copied. Media files stay on Steam's CDN; only Steam CDN addresses are accepted. A dock with the store link and favorite appears once the title card scrolls away (always on phones). Text is at least 12px. Five warm palettes respond to the first six published tags and the genres, so a low-voted tag does not set the mood. The native image dialog supports keyboard paging and closing and restores scroll state.
- The labels beneath the game title are Steam genres, explicitly captioned 遊戲類型. They preserve Steam's order (up to four), rather than being selected from user tags. The discovery TAG chips come from Steam's popular user-defined tags. Official Taiwan Store labels override the local translation fallback; canonical English tag names and Steam tag IDs keep existing discovery links and shared-tag matching intact. Genre translations are kept separate from TAG translations. Hero TAG chips link directly to discovery. In the lower discovery section, sidebar TAG buttons preview up to three matching games in place; 綜合推薦 resets the preview. Counts exclude the current game and use the same candidate set as the preview. One contextual link beside the results opens the selected TAG's discovery page, or all TAGs when no TAG is selected. Selected buttons expose their state, keyboard focus stays in place, and transitions respect the shared motion preference.
- `explore.html` filters all published eligible upcoming games, beyond the 45-day shortlist. Tag search accepts English or translated labels; name, followers, sort and local favorite filters combine with the selected tag. Detail pages retain the referring list's filters on return and offer a retry when public JSON cannot load.
- Language badges preserve independent Traditional/Simplified support and existing English/other/unknown fallbacks. Display-name script conversion never changes a game's actual language support.
- The shared motion preference (`src/shared/state/motion.ts`) works across all pages and tabs. The device's reduced-motion preference always wins. Motion is limited to short card entrances, a single brief background entrance, hover feedback, month changes and favorite feedback; decorative movement does not loop. The explicitly requested activity ticker moves source-backed updates, pauses on hover/reading/background tabs and has a dedicated pause button, full-list dialog and static reduced-motion fallback.
- Search supports localized titles, original titles and AppID. Home search applies to the selected month. Follower filters, sort order and calendar state are represented in the URL. `/` focuses search and Escape clears it.
- Favorites use localStorage under `game-trend-radar:saved:v1`, with cross-tab updates and session-only fallback if storage is restricted. Favorites are local to the browser, not Steam follows or wishlists. Missing records retain their favorite IDs but are not shown as current data.
- Lists render 36 records at a time. Loading, empty, search-empty, unavailable-data and unavailable-cover states are distinct. The data-loading retry works without reloading the whole page.
- Focus indicators, semantic labels, skip navigation, native controls and reduced-motion preferences are supported. Game data is inserted with `textContent`; only a constant authored SVG icon uses `innerHTML`.

## Data boundary

Existing admission thresholds and candidate workflows are unchanged. The additive insights builder reads accepted public records and publishes activity/history JSON; it never fills missing measurements or invents dates. `src/data/storage.ts` reads the public index/month shards and per-AppID records, with the existing legacy JSON fallback. The data adapter retains current title, image, follower and recent-release rules. Discovery enriches only those accepted records, respects explicit empty metadata, and never infers tags from genres. Dates remain in Asia/Taipei. The detail page merges the AppID record over the corresponding catalog record so missing optional metadata can still be read from the catalog.

## Discovery and observations (2026-09-28)

- Multi-TAG discovery supports repeated `tag` parameters, ALL/ANY matching, repeated `exclude` parameters and actual supported-language filters. Existing single-TAG URLs remain valid. Counts beside candidate tags describe the entire accepted discovery pool; combined result counts appear with the games.
- The update ticker reports observed new admissions and release-date changes for the past 14 days. Initial catalog import is a baseline, not a fictitious batch of newly discovered games. Open the full list to read without movement.
- Growth uses dated official Followers observations. The leaderboard ranks 7- and 30-day changes: the baseline is the observation nearest to 7 or 30 days before the latest one (within 3 or 7 days), and the change is scaled to exactly 7 or 30 days. A latest measurement older than eight days does not rank; unavailable, stale and insufficient-history states remain distinct. Tracking ends on release day, whose measurement is the final Followers count; the leaderboard also limits future releases to one year. Archived games remain in the catalog.
- `scripts/build_radar_insights.py` updates three public files hourly at minute 17. The backend `steam-public-growth.yml` job is dispatched at 01:15, 07:15, 13:15 and 19:15 Taipei until that day succeeds, shares the official-collector concurrency lock, uses at most 120 requests spaced at least 30 seconds apart and stops immediately on HTTP 429. Accepted games follow the shared Followers cadence: every 7 days until 14 days before release, every 2 days within those 14 days, and once on release day; a game not yet due is reported as scheduled with its last real measurement. Accepted historical snapshots were replayed with their source check times. This yields sparse authentic history, not a fabricated populated leaderboard.
- Experimental comparison is a separate page with 2–3 accepted games, local browser selection and shareable `ids` URLs. Daily scheduled history is the main comparison: all games share a 7/30/90-day window, an explicit cutoff date and a common chart scale. Total Followers, adjacent-day net changes and baseline growth rates preserve missing days as gaps. Current catalog counters never fill history. Comparison metrics require the same exact endpoints; archived history stays available. Current date/language/TAG/description metadata is in a collapsed reference section; at most three descriptions load only when expanded.
- Lists retain filters, loaded page size and scroll position when returning from profiles. Shared navigation transitions are progressive enhancements with a motion-preference fallback. Original calendar, rocket and TAG drawings also make empty states useful.
- Modules: `src/features/insights/` (date/metric rules, growth views and styles), `src/shared/interactions/` (ticker, comparison tray, return state) and `src/features/comparison/` (comparison history and reference).
- Checks additionally include `node --test tests/insights.test.cjs` and `python3 -m unittest discover -s tests -p test_radar_insights.py`; responsive harness options include growth and comparison.

### Historical comparison clarification

`src/features/comparison/history.js` consumes only histories from `data/growth.json`. The default cutoff is the newest valid saved observation among the selected games, visibly labeled; it is never relabeled as today. Changing the period, cutoff or metric reuses the loaded daily data. The shareable URL stores IDs, period, resolved cutoff and metric. Missing days break the SVG path and cannot produce a daily delta; a zero baseline cannot produce a percentage. All values can be read with a keyboard-operated date slider. Legend switches recalculate the visible chart scale. Old games stop recording after release + 30 days but can be inspected using a historical cutoff. The separate live growth leaderboard retains its freshness rule.



## Twitch observation experiment (2026-09-29)

`twitch.html` is a standalone, static-JSON observation notebook, linked from every current navigation disclosure. Lavender paper, mint/peach evidence cards and an original television drawing extend the existing identity without a promotional hero. The responsive list pairs three real metrics with three independently labelled signals. Native details exposes provenance, reference checks and lazy hourly history; native controls, search composition, focus, clear states and reduced motion remain supported.

`src/features/twitch/snapshot.js` validates and normalizes legacy and schema-v2 Twitch snapshots. Legacy top-stream samples are never admitted to any candidate/game list; the UI displays a waiting state until schema-v2 data arrives. Their median and NEW observations remain unavailable. All schema-v2 candidates at the configured threshold are considered, including pending verification, rather than only confirmed top_games. Official observations retain their timestamps/expiry; source-specific predictions are read as saved, not recalculated in the browser. Historical absence is not zero, fractional medians survive, and future samples cannot fill older snapshots. The primary median and its sorting use only complete filtered_audience records under followers_gt_1000_viewers_gte_10_v1 (Followers > 1,000, viewers >= 10, followers no older than 24 hours). Each row names the eligible sample size; partial queries, no samples, mismatched rules and old records never fall back to the unfiltered median. Expanded details show exclusion counts and the separately labelled all-channel reference. Game viewer/streamer totals and admission stay unfiltered; hourly history follows the same separation. History uses at most two Taipei-day JSON files on demand, cached across opened rows.

No catalog, secret, Twitch API or private endpoint is used. Game names and source text enter the DOM through textContent. Outbound evidence URLs must be HTTPS; artwork is limited to the published Twitch CDN host and has a letter fallback. Filters persist in the URL, list nodes are reused, results load 24 at a time and images load lazily. `tests/twitch.test.cjs` covers source distinctions, missing values, date boundaries, thresholds, unsafe URLs and history gaps.


### Interactive hourly history (2026-09-30)

The per-game history table is replaced by a responsive native SVG line chart, placed before provenance details for quicker access. Three metric buttons select separate zero-based scales: lavender viewers, mint streamers, and warm orange follower-qualified median. A 6/24-hour range control, mouse crosshair, touch selection, and a keyboard-accessible range slider update a persistent value inspector. Hour buckets and actual snapshot times are separately labelled in Taipei time. Paths split at every missing/absent/null slot; single observations remain visible as points, actual zero stays zero, and partial or pre-filter medians never enter the filtered series. Retry and snapshot replacement release previous observers/listeners. No chart library, animation loop or extra API request is introduced. Desktop and mobile checks cover gaps, legacy/partial metrics, single points, empty charts, range/metric switches, pointer/keyboard/touch selection and failed-history retry.

### Candidate admission correction

The Twitch page defaults to positive NEW evidence: a dated official NEW observation or an explicitly positive Twitch/IGDB date prediction. Popularity alone, unknown status and a negative date prediction cannot admit a game into that view. All-candidate and pending views require explicit selection, use contextual headings and explain their verification status. Legacy samples remain excluded even through shared all-candidate/search URLs. Predictions retain their experimental source labels; no backend thresholds or historical records are changed. Regression checks cover League of Legends as a popular, unverified candidate with an older IGDB date.
